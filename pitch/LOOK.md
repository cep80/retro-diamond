# Diamond Shine — Look Bible

**Test:** fill her black. Can you name her?

**House:** Cygames pretty-athlete key visual. She looks like a Cygames winning still in a real baseball uniform — merch-ready, not a TV episode, not a pinup, not a sports-manga boy with lashes. Finish, line, eyes, and refuse list: [`STYLE.md`](STYLE.md). This file is kishitsu only.

Uma variation is not horse ears. It is height, hair mass, face roundness, and one inevitable prop. Baseball constraint: no ears, no tails, no concerts.

**Cast is girls only.** Six young women. Feminine anime faces. No male-presenting characters, no androgynous boy-clone stock, no “tomboy = boy cut” shortcuts that read male in a group still.

- **Complex / practice** = shared cream Skyline practice whites. Identity is body + hair + how the cap sits.
- **Game day** = unique kit silhouette. Still a baseball uniform. Not a recolor of Aoi.

**Hard art rules**

- Exactly one bat or zero. If a bat exists, both knob and barrel are in front of the torso. Nothing intersects the spine or ponytail.
- **Group / hero shots: zero bats.** Props get weird at small scale.
- お立ち台 / Curtain Call = cap in the hand, **no bat**.
- Five fingers per hand. Gloves have a thumb.
- **Glove rule:** empty closed mitt, OR a baseball held in a **bare hand**. Never a ball sitting in the webbing (AI melts hand + glove + ball). Never fingerless “tactical” gloves as a mitt.
- No letters, logos, or gibberish on bats, caps, or jerseys except a readable number: 1 / 18 / 4 / 21 / 99 / 2.
- Do not use old portraits as image references. They bake in same-face.
- **No cowbell prop.** Miki’s walk-up may still be *named* Cowbell in audio; it is not a belt charm.
