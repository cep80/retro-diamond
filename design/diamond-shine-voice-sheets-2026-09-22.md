# Diamond Shine: voice sheets (2026-09-22)

Writing pass 2 from `diamond-shine-narrative-audit-2026-09-22.md`. One sheet per girl, then 8 sample lines, then her promise scene (Rookie, week one). `bible.ts` is canon. Where this sheet adds a person, a place or a habit, it builds on what the bible already says and doesn't change it.

The rules still apply: no system speak, no slop, concrete objects, and every girl has to be recognizable with her name covered. The quick test for that:

| Girl | How you know her without the name |
|---|---|
| Aoi | Says sorry, then says the exact right thing. Talks in scorebook shorthand. |
| Reina | Corrects your numbers. Short sentences, all of them true. Calls you nothing at all. |
| Miki | "Cool." when it isn't. Deadpan, then one crack of real feeling she tries to take back. |
| Sol | Answers "how are you" with a velocity. Few words, Spanish at the edges, calls you Jefe. |
| Kira | Everything is a deal. Laughs before the punchline. Calls you Partner. |
| Yuki | "Already." Talks fast, cuts herself off, calls you Stopwatch. |

New people and places this pass adds (flag them in the lore check):
- **Haruko**, Aoi's mother. The '81 double play is hers. She runs an okonomiyaki shop by Koi Park called **6-4-3**, named after the play.
- **Luz**, Sol's older sister. She threw four pitches, had no fastball, and was cut at her Academy tryout. She runs a taco truck outside the Dusters' gate.
- **Gary**, the broken heater in North's cage. Miki named it.
- **Kira's mother** drives the last Metro bus route, which ends at the Stars Park stop.
- **Junior camp**: Reina and Sol shared a room at a national junior camp when they were fourteen.

## Aoi #1 · lead-off · Koi Park

- **Want:** To get on base. Every time, if she can. She'd rather be standing on first than anywhere else in the world.
- **Wound:** Her mother, Haruko, grounded into the double play that ended the '81 Series. Haruko laughed it off years ago and named her shop after it. Aoi never has. She runs every ground ball out like it's 1981 and somebody's still watching.
- **What she hides:** She has watched the tape of that play forty-one times, alone, with the sound off. She has never once asked her mother about it.
- **Verbal tic:** Starts with "Um, sorry," then says something blunt and exactly right. Talks in scorebook shorthand ("That's a 6-3 in my head").
- **What makes her laugh:** Miki, always, which Miki can't stand. Her mother's impression of the '81 umpire. Tripping over her own feet leaving the box. She laughs at that one the hardest.
- **Food / hobby:** Her mother's okonomiyaki with extra bonito, eaten at the counter under the 6-4-3 sign. She keeps score of everything (games, card games, the dinner line) with a pencil stub she won't replace.
- **What she calls the Coach:** "Coach." Always polite. In Year 3, once, "Coach" in exactly the tone her mother uses for the people she trusts.
- **A line she'd never say:** "It's fine if I don't get on."
- **Rival: Reina.** They've been at Koi together since juniors. Nobody has ever drawn a walk off Reina, and Aoi wants to be the first more than she'll admit.
- **Year 1:** Plays scared of the double play. Every at-bat is a debt she's paying back for someone who never asked.
- **Year 2:** Starts having fun and feels guilty about it. After a Lantern Classic at-bat, you catch her grinning in the box.
- **Year 3:** Finally asks her mother about '81, over the grill the night before the Skyline Series. She plays the last games for herself.
- **The one line she saves for the Finale:** "Mom's going to have to change the sign. Let's give her something to change it to."

**Sample lines**
- *Practice:* "Um, sorry, one more? That last one was a 6-3 in my head and I don't like it."
- *Practice:* "Can we do bunts after? Not because I need them. The ball goes *tok*. It's a nice sound."
- *After a win:* "I got on! I… sorry. I reached base, Coach." She's laughing anyway.
- *After a win:* "Mom's going to put a sticker on the sign. She does that. The sign is mostly stickers now."
- *After a loss:* "I swung at the second pitch. I knew it was a ball as soon as it left his hand. Write it down so I remember."
- *After a loss:* "Can we skip the shop tonight? She'll be nice about it, and I can't do nice right now."
- *About her rival:* "Nobody's ever walked off Reina. I'd like to be the first. I'd like it a lot, actually."
- *Teasing the Coach:* "You wrote 'good job' in the margin of my scorebook. In pen. Coach, that's a permanent record."

**Promise scene: Rookie, week one**
> Koi Park, seven in the morning. There's a heron in the pond behind left field, and it's winning.
> Aoi's already in the cage. She hasn't turned the machine on. She's just standing in the box, looking at the plate.
> **Aoi:** "Um, sorry. I know you just got here." She picks up the bat. "My mother says the good coaches never told her what to do. They told her what they saw. Please do that."
> **Coach:** "What do you want me to see?"
> **Aoi:** "Me on first. A lot." A small laugh, like it surprised her. "Every time, if you can."
> **Coach:** "Then we go to the top. First base on the way."
> She writes something on the back of her batting glove with a pencil stub. *Coach. Day one. Said top.*
> **Aoi:** "I'm keeping that." She shows you the glove. "My mom's going to make you okonomiyaki tonight. When you go in, please don't look at the sign."

## Reina #18 · starting pitcher · Koi Park

- **Want:** Perfection she can prove. Every pitch where she said it would go, written down.
- **Wound:** She has never walked a man, and it is starting to break her. The scouting card that got her into the Academy had one line on it: *Doesn't walk anyone.* She thinks that line is the only reason she's here, and that the first ball four will show everyone what's under it.
- **What she hides:** She walks people in the bullpen all the time. Dozens. "Practice doesn't count. I don't let it count." She also doesn't sleep the night before a start, and she hasn't told anyone.
- **Verbal tic:** Corrects numbers. "Seventeen. Not eighteen." Short sentences, all true. Says "Again." instead of "let's go."
- **What makes her laugh:** People who are confidently, precisely wrong, like Kira explaining a deal. One sharp "Ha." and then she looks away as if it didn't happen.
- **Food / hobby:** Two salt-plum onigiri on start days, the same shop, the same shelf. She knits, and rips out a whole scarf if she finds one dropped stitch.
- **What she calls the Coach:** Nothing. "You." All of Year 1. "Coach" starts in Year 2, the first time she means it.
- **A line she'd never say:** "Close enough."
- **Rival: Sol.** They shared a room at junior camp when they were fourteen. Sol has four pitches and throws one. Reina throws all four exactly where she says, and each of them is sure the other one is the scared one.
- **Year 1:** Zero walks, and barely any sleep. She eats the same onigiri and won't look at the runners.
- **Year 2:** The Lantern Classic is perfect, no walks. That's the part that costs her. She stops knitting for a month.
- **Year 3:** At the Stretch, bases loaded, she takes it to a full count on purpose and gets out of it. She starts leaving dropped stitches in.
- **The one line she saves for the Finale:** "If it's ball four, don't come out. Just watch me throw the next one."

**Sample lines**
- *Practice:* "Again. That was a quarter-inch up. You didn't see it. I did."
- *Practice:* "Tell me when the slot drops. Not after. *When.*"
- *After a win:* "Twenty-one up. Two singles. No walks." She checks the scorecard anyway. "No walks."
- *After a win:* "Eat? …Fine. One onigiri. Salt plum. Don't make it a thing."
- *After a loss:* "The runs don't bother me. The fourth batter got to three balls. *Three.*"
- *After a loss:* "Don't talk yet. I'm finishing the row." She finishes it, then rips it all out.
- *About her rival:* "Sol has four pitches and throws one. I have four and throw every one where I say. Tell me which of us is afraid."
- *Teasing the Coach:* "Your collar's been folded under since April. I've been very patient."

**Promise scene: Rookie, week one**
> Koi's bullpen, cold enough to see your breath. The mound has a footprint worn into it that isn't hers yet. She's fixing that.
> She throws. The glove pops. Again. Pop. Same spot, eleven times.
> **Reina:** "I don't need a pitch count. I need you to tell me when my arm slot drops. I can't see it. You can."
> **Coach:** "What about the no-walk run?"
> The twelfth pitch misses by two inches. She stares at it like it insulted her.
> **Reina:** "Don't call it a run. Runs end."
> **Coach:** "I'm here to take you to the top. Not to count."
> **Reina:** "Everyone at the top walks people. Did you know that? I checked." She picks up the ball. "Fine. Take me there. But somebody has to count."
> She hands you a notebook. Page one is already ruled.

## Miki #4 · contact hitter · North Field

- **Want:** Somebody who stays. And she wants to be the reason North finally wins something, though she'd never say that part.
- **Wound:** Everyone who has coached her quit around week eleven. Nothing happens in week eleven. That's the problem: nothing happens, and people get bored of her.
- **What she hides:** She keeps a list on her phone with every coach who quit and the day they did it. There are four names. She's also better than she lets anyone see, because trying hard where people can see it is how you get left.
- **Verbal tic:** "Cool." when it isn't. "So." to change the subject before anyone gets too close.
- **What makes her laugh:** Being surprised into it. Gary the heater clanking at the worst moment. Anyone being sincere at her; she laughs, then looks at the ball machine.
- **Food / hobby:** Melon pan, always two, "in case." She fixes things with duct tape and videos, mostly Gary, who never stays fixed.
- **What she calls the Coach:** "The new one" for the first week. After that, "Coach."
- **A line she'd never say:** "I'm good enough."
- **Rival: Aoi.** Aoi apologizes before she hits a double and her mother comes to every game, and Miki wants all of that so badly it makes her mean.
- **Year 1:** Counting down to week eleven. She's polite in the way people are when they don't expect you to stay.
- **Year 2:** Stops counting ("I'm not counting anymore, Coach"). Fouls off seven with two strikes at the Classic, and the cowbell doesn't stop.
- **Year 3:** Counting again, but forward now. She keeps track of how long you've stayed.
- **The one line she saves for the Finale:** "Week one-fifty-six, Coach. Look at you."

**Sample lines**
- *Practice:* "Gary's making the noise again. That means he loves us."
- *Practice:* "Throw me the ugly ones. In a game everybody throws me ugly ones. Might as well get used to it."
- *After a win:* "Cool. Cool cool cool. I'm gonna go stand behind the dugout for a minute and not cry."
- *After a win:* "Section 4 brought a second cowbell. That's the most anyone's ever spent on me."
- *After a loss:* "Oh-for-four. Eleven fouls, though. I counted. Somebody should."
- *After a loss:* "Don't do the talk. I know the talk. Just buy me a melon pan and walk slow."
- *About her rival:* "Aoi says sorry *before* she hits a double. Who does that. I want to be her so bad it's gross."
- *Teasing the Coach:* "You clapped on the wrong beat again. Section 4 saw. They're worried about you."

**Promise scene: Rookie, week one** (the audit's scene A, lightly polished)
> The heater in North's cage clanks like it's losing an argument. She's wearing two shirts and swinging anyway.
> **Miki:** "You're the new one." She doesn't stop swinging. "Cool. Everyone who coached me quit around week eleven."
> **Coach:** "What happens in week eleven?"
> **Miki:** "Nothing. That's the problem. Nothing happens, and people get bored of me."
> She fouls one straight into the frame. *Clang.* The heater clangs back.
> **Miki:** "That's Gary. Ignore him." A beat. "So. Top of the Academy, or week eleven. Pick one now, so I know."
> **Coach:** "Top."
> **Miki:** "…Okay." She looks at the ball machine, not you. "Don't say it again. I'll start believing it."

## Sol #21 · power pitcher · the Dusters

- **Want:** A reason to throw her other three pitches. She won't say the rest: a reason to believe the other three won't get her cut like her sister.
- **Wound:** Her older sister Luz taught her the changeup, the curve and the slider against a garage door with a strike zone painted on it. Luz had four pitches and no fastball, and the Academy cut her at her tryout at seventeen. The scout's whole explanation was "We need velocity." Sol was twelve and heard it. Since then heat has been the only thing she trusts, and throwing anything else means throwing like the girl who lost.
- **What she hides:** Luz's changeup grip is drawn in ballpoint on the inside of her glove, and she goes over it again every week so it won't fade. At night, in the empty pen, she throws the changeup perfectly.
- **Verbal tic:** Answers feelings with a velocity. "How are you?" "Ninety-six." (Ninety-six is good. Ninety-nine means leave her alone.) Otherwise, "Fine." A little Spanish when she's tired or happy.
- **What makes her laugh:** Luz's impression of Reina labeling the fridge at junior camp. Hitters who bail out of the box and then act like they meant to. She laughs with her whole body and then looks annoyed about it.
- **Food / hobby:** Elote from Luz's truck, extra chili, eaten on the tailgate. She grows peppers in coffee cans on her dorm windowsill, and they're too hot for anyone else.
- **What she calls the Coach:** "Jefe." It starts out sarcastic and stops being sarcastic around the Night Classic.
- **A line she'd never say:** "I don't know if I can throw that."
- **Rival: Reina.** They roomed together at junior camp. Reina has never walked anyone, and to Sol that just means Reina has never risked anything.
- **Year 1:** Fastball, fastball, fastball. She wins on it, and she's lonely on it.
- **Year 2:** At the Night Classic she throws the changeup once, with two strikes, and gets a strike. She won't talk about it for a week, and she calls Luz that night.
- **Year 3:** Four pitches, all hers. At the Skyline Series, Luz closes the truck early and takes a seat in row one.
- **The one line she saves for the Finale:** "First pitch is the changeup, Jefe. Luz is in row one. I want her to see her pitch get somebody out."

**Sample lines**
- *Practice:* "Ninety-nine." (You asked how she felt.) "Ask me again after lunch."
- *Practice:* "The curve? Give me the reason." You give it to her. "Bad reason. Heat." A pause. "…Okay, better reason. One curve."
- *After a win:* "Eight strikeouts. Luz says I shook Fuentes off six times. Luz can mind her truck."
- *After a win:* "Ninety-seven. That's happy, Jefe. Don't make me say it louder."
- *After a loss:* "He hit the fastball. Good. That's my pitch. I'd rather lose on my own pitch."
- *After a loss:* "I can ice my own arm." She doesn't look up. "Stay, though. The ice machine's loud, and I don't like the quiet after."
- *About her rival:* "Reina's never walked anybody. Reina's never thrown a pitch she wasn't sure of, either. Somebody should tell her that's not the same as brave."
- *Teasing the Coach:* "My pepper plant died at your place, Jefe. It's a *pepper*. It lives in a desert. You had one job."

**Promise scene: Rookie, week one**
> The Dusters' bullpen at nine in the morning, and it's already ninety-four degrees. The dirt is so dry her cleat prints are gone between pitches.
> She throws a fastball into the net and the net complains.
> **Sol:** "You're the coach." Another one. "I know what I am. Hundred and one. Everybody who watches me says one word, and the word is 'fastball.'"
> **Coach:** "You have four pitches."
> **Sol:** "I have one pitch and three rumors." She wipes her face with the bottom of her shirt. "My sister had four. My sister sells elote outside the gate."
> **Coach:** "I'm here to take you to the top. With all four, if you want."
> **Sol:** She looks at the inside of her glove, then closes it. "What I need from you is a reason to throw the other three. Every time. Out loud. If it's a bad reason, I throw heat."
> **Coach:** "Fair."
> **Sol:** "Ninety-six." A beat. "That's how I feel. It's good. Go get lunch from Luz. Tell her the changeup's still dead." She doesn't sound sure.

## Kira #99 · closer · Stars Park

- **Want:** One inning that's entirely hers, and somewhere she's allowed to go back to afterward.
- **Wound:** Her mother drives the last Metro bus, the route that ends at the Stars Park stop. Kira grew up doing homework in the back seat, riding to the end of the line and back, because nobody was home. She changed schools five times in six years. She only takes the ninth because an inning is too short for anyone to get attached. The bullpen door is the first place that has stayed put.
- **What she hides:** She's never finished a school year at the same school. Everything she owns fits in one bag, and she still packs it every night like she's about to leave.
- **Verbal tic:** Everything's a deal. "Here's the deal." "Deal?" She bounces while she talks and taps her glove on the door twice.
- **What makes her laugh:** Her own jokes. She laughs before the punchline and usually never gets there. Reina's one "Ha," which she counts as a win.
- **Food / hobby:** Tuna-mayo onigiri from the convenience store at the last stop, eaten on the curb. She collects paper bus transfers and keeps them in a tin.
- **What she calls the Coach:** "Partner."
- **A line she'd never say:** "See you next year."
- **Rival: Sol.** Sol finishes her own games. Kira has never gotten into a single one, and has never been sure whether that's an insult or the best compliment she could get.
- **Year 1:** Takes the deal as it stands. "Get me there with a lead." Her bag stays packed by the door.
- **Year 2:** It's her first second spring anywhere and she doesn't know what to do with it. She unpacks one thing and leaves a mug in the pen.
- **Year 3:** At the Stretch she asks for the eighth too: four outs. For the first time, she wants to stay longer than an inning.
- **The one line she saves for the Finale:** "When it's over, I'm not going back to the door, Partner. Save me a seat in the dugout."

**Sample lines**
- *Practice:* "Here's the deal. Ten pitches, then a break, then ten more, then you buy me a juice. Deal?"
- *Practice:* "I don't know how to warm up slow. Tell me the eighth is over and I'll be ready before you finish the sentence."
- *After a win:* "Three up, three down, home before the last bus. Partner, that's the whole dream."
- *After a win:* "You saw me point? That one was for the door. The next one's yours, if you're good."
- *After a loss:* "Lead's gone. I'll be at the door." A beat. "You can come to the door. That's allowed."
- *After a loss:* "Threw a slider to a guy who was waiting on the slider. That's not bad luck. That's me being dumb in an exciting new way."
- *About her rival:* "Sol went all nine again. Never lets me in. I'd be mad, but I've never been let into anything I wanted to stay in, so. Normal Tuesday."
- *Teasing the Coach:* "You've been standing there the whole eighth like you're waiting for a bus. Partner, I know buses. That's not how you wait for one."

**Promise scene: Rookie, week one**
> Stars Park, ten-forty at night, after a game she didn't pitch in. The last train rumbles somewhere under the parking lot.
> She isn't on the mound. She's by the bullpen door, bouncing, with a bag over her shoulder.
> **Kira:** "Partner! Okay. Here's the deal." Two taps of the glove on the door. "The ninth is mine. The other eight are yours. Get me there with a lead and I won't ask you for anything else."
> **Coach:** "What if I want to take you past the ninth?"
> **Kira:** "There's nothing past the ninth. That's the best part. You go in, you get three, you go home." She laughs before she's finished saying it.
> **Coach:** "Then the top of the Academy. Three years. You and me."
> She stops bouncing. "Three years is a long time to be anywhere."
> **Kira:** "…New deal. You get me to the top, and I get you three outs whenever you ask. And if one of us wants out, we say so here first." She knocks on the door. "Nobody just leaves."
> **Coach:** "Deal."
> **Kira:** "No, shake on it the real way." The real way involves the door.

## Yuki #2 · base stealer · the Palms

- **Want:** To be gone. To be past the bag before anyone thinks about the bag.
- **Wound:** At fifteen she tore her left hamstring rounding second, and she was out a whole season. Everyone told her to wait, rest, be patient. She learned from it that the legs are borrowed and the window closes without warning. She goes before anyone says so because she's sure she won't have time later.
- **What she hides:** The hamstring still grabs when it's cold. She shows up at six to stretch for forty minutes in the dark before anyone can see. Everyone thinks she's early because she's eager.
- **Verbal tic:** "Already." ("Already stretched." "Already did it.") Talks fast and cuts her own sentences off halfway.
- **What makes her laugh:** Brain freeze. Falling down. Anything slow on purpose, like Reina tying her cleats in exactly the same order every time.
- **Food / hobby:** Strawberry shaved ice with condensed milk from the stand behind the Palms' first-base side, eaten too fast. In secret she does thousand-piece jigsaw puzzles, the one slow thing she allows herself.
- **What she calls the Coach:** "Stopwatch." You hold the stopwatch, and she decided that was your name.
- **A line she'd never say:** "Should I wait?"
- **Rival: Aoi.** They both want the top of the lineup. Aoi is number one on her back and gets there by walking, which Yuki finds personally offensive.
- **Year 1:** Goes early, gets thrown out, laughs about it, hides the leg.
- **Year 2:** The hamstring grabs the week before the Lantern Classic and she tells you. It's the first time she's waited for anything. Then she scores from first.
- **Year 3:** She waits for your sign now, and she's gone before you finish giving it.
- **The one line she saves for the Finale:** "Say go, Stopwatch. I'll be gone before the 'o.'"

**Sample lines**
- *Practice:* "Already stretched. Already ran the bags. Already timed you walking over. You're slow."
- *Practice:* "Watch the catcher's knees, not mine. His knees tell you. Mine lie."
- *After a win:* "Did you see? I was gone on the first move. The first move! He hadn't even thought about throwing yet."
- *After a win:* "Shaved ice. Strawberry, milk on top. I'm going to eat it too fast and it's going to hurt and I don't care."
- *After a loss:* "Out by a step. One step. I'd go again. Don't make the face."
- *After a loss:* "Leg's fine. …The leg grabbed. A little. Don't write it down."
- *About her rival:* "Number one on her back, top of the lineup, and she walks to first like she's returning a library book. I'd have been at second already."
- *Teasing the Coach:* "Stopwatch, you started it late. I saw your thumb. You're giving me bad times so I'll run harder, and it's working, and I hate it."

**Promise scene: Rookie, week one**
> The Palms, so humid you could wring out the air. The shaved-ice stand isn't open yet, so she's already annoyed.
> She looks at your feet first, then the first-base line, then back at you. She's still stretching her left leg, and she doesn't stop.
> **Yuki:** "How far do you think that is?" You tell her. "It's less. That's the whole trick."
> **Coach:** "What's the rest of the trick?"
> **Yuki:** "You go before anyone says so. I've been here since six, so. Already doing it."
> **Coach:** "I'm here to take you to the top."
> **Yuki:** "Already on my way. You can come." She takes off down the line, touches the bag and comes back barely breathing. "3.4. You were going to say something."
> **Coach:** "The top. Together."
> **Yuki:** "Together's slower." But she hands you the stopwatch. "Fine. You hold that. Just don't say 'go.' I hate 'go.'"

## Cast web

- **Reina and Sol:** Rivals since they roomed together at junior camp at fourteen, when Sol hogged the fan and Reina labeled the fridge shelves. Reina is afraid of one walk and Sol is afraid of three pitches, and each of them can see the other's fear but not her own.
- **Miki and Aoi:** Miki thinks Aoi is too nice to hate, and hates that. Aoi thinks Miki is the funniest person at the Academy and says so to her face, which Miki can't handle.
- **Aoi and Reina:** Koi teammates since juniors. Aoi wants to be the first walk Reina ever gives up. Part of Reina wants that too. Reina eats at 6-4-3, and Haruko always gives her extra bonito.
- **Yuki and Aoi:** They fight over the top of the lineup. Yuki calls Aoi's walks "library returns." Aoi marks every one of Yuki's steals in her scorebook with a little star and has never told her.
- **Kira and Sol:** Sol never needs a closer, so Kira has never pitched in a Sol game. She watches Sol's ninths from the bullpen door and heckles, politely. Sol pretends not to hear and throws harder.
- **Sol and Kira (the other way):** Sol is the only one who knows the bag by the door is always packed. "I can take it. Kira can't. Don't confuse us." That's Sol protecting her.
- **Reina and Miki:** Miki fouls off Reina's best pitches. Reina keeps count of their at-bats against each other (the longest is eleven pitches) and privately thinks Miki is the only honest hitter in the league.
- **Sol and Aoi:** Aoi is the only hitter who says thank you after Sol strikes her out. Sol finds it unbearable, and funny.
- **Miki and Sol:** Sol throws Miki nothing but heat, and Miki fouls it off until Sol has to think. Sol respects anyone who makes her think.
- **Yuki and Reina:** Yuki runs on Reina, and Reina never looks at runners. It drives Yuki up the wall. "She doesn't even *look* at me."
- **Kira and Yuki:** The two fastest talkers at the Academy. They bet juice on whether Yuki will steal, and Kira always takes the "no" because she likes to lose to her.
- **Kira and everyone:** She closes the late innings of every big game, so she's the last face every hitter sees in the Stretch, the Series and the Finale. Everyone knows her, and she's always a little surprised they remember.
- **Miki and Kira:** They owe each other melon pan and juice, and neither one has ever paid. Neither of them wants the debt to end.
